Research project

Competing for AI Attention: Firm Visibility in Generative AI Search

Chengzi (Charles) ZHU
In progress
  • RQ: As generative AI emerges as a new information intermediary, does it create new opportunities for startups to gain market visibility, or reinforce the advantages of established firms?
  • Compiled and preliminarily analyzed a daily panel dataset tracking firm-level exposure within industries across different countries and generative AI platforms (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek), covering data from February 2026 onward and including visibility (firm mention frequency), sentiment (evaluation favorability), citations (cited sources), and advertising exposure (ad appearances).
  • Preliminary analyses suggest substantial concentration in AI-generated firm visibility across most industries, with the top three firms accounting for approximately 40–70% of total mentions.
  • Next step: Link AI visibility measures to firm-level business outcomes, including sales, revenue, and financial performance, to assess whether visibility in generative-AI search translates into market outcomes.
